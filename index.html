🔲

MICROCONTROLLER

ATmega328P running at 16 MHz handles signal modulation using on-chip PWM timers. OOK (On-Off Keying) encoding converts digital bits into light pulses at up to 500 kbps.

⚙️

DRIVER CIRCUIT

N-channel MOSFET (IRF540N) switches the LED array with microsecond precision. Gate resistor and flyback diode protect the MCU I/O pins from transient spikes.

💡

LED TRANSMITTER

1W high-brightness white LED array driven at 350 mA. Optical lens focuses beam to 30° cone, maximizing intensity and reducing ambient light interference at receiver.

🔆

OPTICAL CHANNEL

Free-space light propagation. Performance varies with distance (inverse-square law), alignment, ambient light level, and surface reflections in the environment.

📡

PHOTODIODE RECEIVER

BPW34 silicon PIN photodiode with 850nm peak sensitivity. Reverse-biased to reduce junction capacitance and improve switching speed to ≥1 MHz.

📺

DISPLAY MODULE

16×2 LCD via I2C expander displays received message, BER, packet count, and signal quality in real time without needing a host computer.
